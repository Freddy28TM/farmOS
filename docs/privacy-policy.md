# FarmOS Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 9, 2026

## 1. Introduction

FarmOS is an agricultural decision-support system designed to help users interpret environmental conditions in the context of farm information and produce explainable risk assessments.

This Privacy Policy explains how FarmOS may collect, use, store, protect, and handle information within the current MVP and in planned future versions.

FarmOS is designed around the principle of collecting and using information only where it is necessary to provide the intended service.

---

## 2. Information FarmOS Handles

The current FarmOS application may handle the following categories of information.

### 2.1 Account Information

For registered users, the system may store information required to create and authenticate an account.

This may include:

* Username or account identifier
* Authentication information
* Secure password hash

Passwords are not intended to be stored as plaintext.

---

### 2.2 Farm Information

Users may provide information about their farms, including:

* Farm name
* Farm location
* Latitude
* Longitude
* Crop
* Crop growth stage

This information is used to provide farm-specific decision support.

---

### 2.3 Environmental Information

FarmOS may obtain environmental information from external environmental-data providers.

For the current MVP, environmental information is obtained using the farm's geographic location.

The current water-risk workflow uses information such as:

* Recent rainfall
* Forecast rainfall
* Temperature

Environmental information is used to generate the risk assessment.

---

### 2.4 Risk Assessment Information

FarmOS may store information generated by the decision engine, including:

* Risk assessment
* Risk level
* Risk score
* Confidence classification
* Contributing factors
* Recommendation
* Explanation
* Assessment timestamp

This information allows assessment history to be maintained.

---

### 2.5 Feedback Information

FarmOS supports feedback associated with risk assessments.

Feedback may include:

* Farmer action
* Observed result
* Associated assessment
* Feedback timestamp

Feedback is stored as historical information and does not automatically modify the decision engine.

---

## 3. How Information Is Used

FarmOS uses information for purposes including:

* Creating and authenticating user accounts
* Associating farms with their owners
* Providing farm-specific environmental information
* Generating risk assessments
* Explaining assessment results
* Maintaining assessment history
* Recording farmer feedback
* Testing and improving the reliability of the application
* Maintaining system security

FarmOS does not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the application's intended functionality without appropriate notice.

---

## 4. Farm Location Information

Farm location is an important part of the current FarmOS workflow because environmental information is associated with a farm's geographic location.

Location information may be used to:

1. Identify the environmental conditions relevant to a farm.
2. Request environmental information from the configured environmental-data provider.
3. Associate environmental conditions with the corresponding farm assessment.

Users should provide only location information necessary for the intended farm assessment.

FarmOS should not be understood as providing continuous real-time tracking of a user's physical location.

---

## 5. Environmental Data Providers

FarmOS currently uses Open-Meteo as its environmental-data provider.

Environmental information may therefore be requested from an external service using the geographic information required to obtain relevant environmental data.

The external provider may process requests according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Future versions of FarmOS may use additional environmental-data providers.

Where providers change, the system should continue to apply appropriate data-minimization and privacy principles.

---

## 6. Authentication and Password Security

FarmOS uses authenticated accounts for protected application resources.

Passwords are protected using password hashing rather than plaintext storage.

Authentication uses access tokens to authorize protected requests.

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential.

FarmOS should never require users to disclose their password to another person through an informal communication channel.

---

## 7. Access to Farm Information

FarmOS is designed around user ownership.

A user's farm information is associated with that user's account.

The backend applies authorization checks to protected resources so that users should only be able to access resources they are authorized to access.

This includes protection of:

* Farm records
* Related risk assessments
* Related feedback

The frontend is not treated as the security boundary. Authorization is enforced by the backend.

---

## 8. Assessment History

FarmOS may retain completed risk assessments so that users can review previous decision-support results.

Assessment history may include:

* Farm context
* Environmental conditions used by the assessment
* Risk level
* Score
* Confidence
* Contributing factors
* Recommendation
* Explanation
* Assessment time

Retaining assessment history supports transparency, evaluation, and future system improvement.

---

## 9. Farmer Feedback

FarmOS allows users to provide feedback about completed assessments.

Feedback may help provide historical information about:

* What action was taken
* What result was observed

The current MVP does not automatically use feedback to retrain or alter the decision engine.

Any future system that uses accumulated feedback for model improvement should apply appropriate validation, evaluation, and privacy safeguards.

---

## 10. Data Sharing

FarmOS is not designed to sell user information.

Information may be processed or transmitted where necessary to provide the application's functionality.

Examples include:

* Environmental-data requests
* Application infrastructure
* Authentication and application services

Any future third-party service integrations should be evaluated for their security, privacy, and data-processing implications.

FarmOS should not disclose user information to unrelated third parties for purposes outside the application's intended functionality without an appropriate legal or user-facing basis.

---

## 11. Data Security

FarmOS is designed with security controls including:

* Password hashing
* Authenticated access
* Authorization checks
* Farm ownership checks
* Input validation
* Separation of frontend and backend security responsibilities

No software system can guarantee absolute security.

Future production deployments should introduce additional controls appropriate to the deployment environment, including:

* HTTPS
* Secure secret management
* Secure token handling
* Rate limiting
* Monitoring
* Security logging
* Database security
* Backup protection
* Access controls
* Regular dependency updates

---

## 12. Data Retention

The current MVP retains application information as required for normal operation and assessment history.

Future production deployments should define specific retention periods based on:

* The type of information
* The purpose for which it is collected
* Legal requirements
* Operational requirements
* User expectations

Data that is no longer necessary should be deleted or appropriately anonymized where practical.

---

## 13. User Rights and Controls

Depending on the applicable legal and deployment context, users may have rights relating to their personal information.

These may include rights to:

* Access information associated with their account
* Request correction of inaccurate information
* Request deletion where applicable
* Ask how information is being used
* Raise privacy concerns

The exact rights available to a user may depend on applicable law and the jurisdiction in which the service operates.

FarmOS should provide appropriate mechanisms for exercising applicable rights in a production deployment.

---

## 14. Children's Privacy

FarmOS is intended as an agricultural decision-support application.

A production deployment should establish an appropriate minimum age and account policy based on its target users, applicable law, and the nature of the service.

Where applicable, additional safeguards should be implemented for younger users.

The current MVP should not be interpreted as establishing a complete production-ready children's privacy framework.

---

## 15. Cookies and Local Storage

The current FarmOS frontend is a static web application.

A production deployment may use browser storage or similar mechanisms to maintain authentication state.

Such mechanisms should be implemented using appropriate security practices.

The current MVP documentation does not claim that a production-grade browser token-storage strategy has been fully implemented.

Future deployments should clearly document any use of:

* Cookies
* Local storage
* Session storage
* Analytics technologies
* Other tracking technologies

---

## 16. Analytics and Tracking

The current FarmOS MVP is not designed around advertising or behavioral tracking.

If analytics or tracking technologies are introduced in a future deployment, users should be informed appropriately and the implementation should follow applicable privacy requirements.

Only information necessary for the stated purpose should be collected.

---

## 17. International and External Data Processing

FarmOS may use external services to obtain environmental information or provide infrastructure.

Depending on the deployment architecture, information may therefore be processed outside the user's local environment or jurisdiction.

Future production deployments should identify applicable data-processing locations and requirements.

## 19. Privacy by Design

FarmOS follows several privacy-by-design principles.

### Data Minimization

Collect and retain only information necessary for the intended functionality.

### Purpose Limitation

Use information for defined application purposes.

### Access Control

Restrict access to information according to authenticated user ownership and authorization.

### Security

Protect information using appropriate technical and organizational safeguards.

### Transparency

Clearly explain what information the system handles and why.

### User Control

Provide appropriate mechanisms for users to review and manage their information.

---

## 20. Responsible Use of Agricultural Information

FarmOS provides decision support based on available environmental information and defined agricultural rules.

Risk assessments and recommendations are not guaranteed predictions of future agricultural outcomes.

Users should consider:

* Local conditions
* Direct farm observations
* Farmer experience
* Appropriate agricultural guidance
* Other relevant environmental information

FarmOS should not be treated as a replacement for professional agricultural judgment.

---

## 21. Current MVP Limitations

This Privacy Policy describes the intended privacy principles of FarmOS, but the current MVP is not a complete production privacy-management platform.

Production deployment may require additional implementation for:

* Formal account-management controls
* Data export
* Data deletion workflows
* Consent management where applicable
* Formal retention schedules
* Privacy request handling
* Production-grade audit logging
* Secure deployment infrastructure
* Formal legal compliance processes

These requirements should be addressed before FarmOS is deployed as a large-scale public service.

```text
User Account
     ↓
Farm Information
     ↓
Farm Location
     ↓
Environmental Data
     ↓
Risk Assessment
     ↓
Assessment History
     ↓
Farmer Feedback
```

The system is designed around:

* Data minimization
* User ownership
* Authentication
* Authorization
* Secure password handling
* Explainability
* Transparency
* Responsible data use

As FarmOS evolves beyond the MVP, its privacy controls and policies should evolve with the system, its users, its external services, and applicable legal requirements.
